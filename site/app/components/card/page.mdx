# Card

<div className="lede">
A card is a surface that says “these things belong together”. It is not a box to put
a border around — if the contents do not form a group, they do not need a card.
</div>

## Slots

`Card` composes from named slots rather than props, so a card with no header simply
doesn't include one. Padding, dividers and the sunken footer all come from tokens,
which is why a card in warehouse mode opens up without anyone changing markup.

<CardDemo code={`
<Card style={{ maxWidth: 420 }}>
  <Card.Header
    title="Batch allocation"
    subtitle="BLR-01 Bommanahalli"
    actions={<Button size="sm" variant="ghost">Edit</Button>}
  />
  <Card.Body>
    Orders are allocated FEFO within the shelf-life constraint. Anything that
    would dispatch below the minimum is held for review.
  </Card.Body>
  <Card.Footer spread>
    <span style={{ color: 'var(--ed-text-muted)', fontSize: 'var(--ed-text-sm)' }}>
      Updated 4 minutes ago
    </span>
    <Button size="sm">Run dry-run</Button>
  </Card.Footer>
</Card>
`} />

## Stats

`Card.Stat` exists because a number in a card is the most common thing in the whole
dashboard, and it was being rebuilt on every screen. The value is set in tabular
figures so a column of them lines up, and `delta` takes its colour from the sign.

<CardDemo code={`
<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: 'repeat(3, 1fr)', gap: 'var(--ed-space-4)', width: '100%' }}>
  <Card><Card.Stat label="Orders today" value="1,284" delta="+8.2%" /></Card>
  <Card><Card.Stat label="Fill rate" value="97.2%" delta="+0.3%" /></Card>
  <Card><Card.Stat label="RTO rate" value="6.8%" delta="-0.5%" /></Card>
</div>
`} />

## Accent

An accent is a left edge in a status colour. Use it when a card's *state* matters at a
glance — a rule that is failing, a shipment that is late. Use it sparingly: a wall of
accented cards says nothing at all.

<CardDemo code={`
<div style={{ display: 'grid', gap: 'var(--ed-space-3)', width: '100%', maxWidth: 420 }}>
  <Card accent="danger"><Card.Body><strong>3 orders</strong> would fail today's rules.</Card.Body></Card>
  <Card accent="warning"><Card.Body><strong>12 put-aways</strong> overdue at BLR-01.</Card.Body></Card>
  <Card accent="success"><Card.Body><strong>All picks</strong> cleared for the 14:00 wave.</Card.Body></Card>
</div>
`} />

## Density and elevation

`dense` tightens padding for cards that sit inside other layouts. `flat` drops the
shadow for a card inside a panel that already has one — stacked elevation reads as
noise rather than depth. `raised` is for a card that genuinely floats.

<CardDemo code={`
<>
  <Card dense style={{ width: 200 }}><Card.Body>Dense</Card.Body></Card>
  <Card flat style={{ width: 200 }}><Card.Body>Flat</Card.Body></Card>
  <Card raised style={{ width: 200 }}><Card.Body>Raised</Card.Body></Card>
</>
`} />

## Interactive cards

A card that navigates should be a link, not a `<div>` with an `onClick`. `interactive`
adds the hover and focus treatment; `as="a"` makes it a real anchor, so middle-click and
open-in-new-tab keep working.

<CardDemo code={`
<Card interactive as="a" href="#" style={{ maxWidth: 360 }}>
  <Card.Header title="Kit build plan" subtitle="BP-001 · 3 kits" />
  <Card.Body>Weekly, every Monday at 06:00.</Card.Body>
</Card>
`} />

## When not to use a card

If everything on the screen is in a card, nothing is grouped — you have just added
borders. Tables do not belong in cards; they have their own surface. And a card
wrapping a single sentence is a paragraph with extra steps.
