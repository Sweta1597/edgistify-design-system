Space

The grid, the control sizes, the corner radii and the shadows. Where colour and type decided how things look, this decides how big they are and how far apart — which is the difference between a dashboard row and a button someone hits wearing a glove.

The grid bends to the product

Base unit 4px, with 2px half-steps kept at the small end. A pure 4px grid is tidier on paper, but 887 of the dashboard’s spacing values are half-steps — 2, 6, 10 and 14px. Rounding a quarter of the existing layout to look neat in a spec is the wrong trade. Half-steps are shaded.

Targets, and the glove

WCAG 2.2 AA asks for 24px, AAA for 44px. Both assume a bare fingertip. A gloved one has a wider and less precise contact patch, so on the warehouse floor 44px is the floor rather than the goal: operational controls are 56px and the primary action on a screen is 64px. The picker app today tops out at 48px, and most of its controls are 36–44px.

RULE 03

A small control can keep its size and still be easy to hit. .ed-target grows the hit area outward with a pseudo-element without changing a single visible pixel — so a 16px icon button in a dense table row still answers to a 24px tap, and 44px in warehouse mode.

Visual size and target size are different problems. Don’t solve the second by inflating the first and losing the row density the dashboard exists for.

Icons sit on the grid too

Lucide draws on a 24px artboard with a 2px stroke, so the rendered stroke is 2 × size ÷ 24. At 13px that lands on 1.08px and at 10px on 0.83px — under one device pixel, which is why small icons in the dashboard look soft. It currently uses seven sizes between 8 and 15px; 198 of 306 icons are off-grid. Five sizes, each with a stroke width that keeps the optical weight constant.

Nesting

An inner radius equals the outer radius minus the padding between them. Repeat the outer value on the inner box and the curves run parallel instead of concentric — the corners look swollen.

Elevation

Four levels, and nothing between. Shadows carry the neutral’s teal cast rather than pure black — a blue-grey shadow over a teal-tinted surface reads as dirt. In dark mode shadows stop working, so elevation is carried by surface lightness with a hairline instead.

Both at once

The same card, the same markup. Only the semantic tokens move — targets from 32px to 56px, icons from 16 to 24, radius from 6 to 10, and card gaps tighten because screen space is scarcer on a handheld than on a monitor.

Dashboard density: 32px controls, 16px icons, 8px vertical cell padding. More rows on screen means fewer scrolls.
